## Adapter behavior

`GameInterface.Services.ObjectReadiness.ICampaignObjectGraphReadiness` is a per-session ledger. `BeginReplicaCreation` records positive evidence of a newly received party; only these objects can be reported as construction-pending. `PublishReplicaWhenReady` retains the original callback. The graph is complete only when the MobileParty, its current PartyBase, ItemRoster and two distinct TroopRosters all have nonzero registered handles, `PartyBase.MobileParty` is the exact party, and both `TroopRoster.OwnerParty` references are the exact current PartyBase.

`ReferencesApplied` inspects current references on the game thread and invokes the original publication callback once. The new `ObjectGraphReadinessUpdatePatch` calls it after the unmodified `GameThread.Update` queue drain. It observes fields as well as setters; it has no timer, delay, work queue replacement or retransmission. `TryEnsureReady` performs the same positive inspection before capture/freeze and reports a nonempty `creation-pending`, `removal-pending` or `publication-fault` reason while a tracked graph remains incomplete. An unknown, untracked or previously committed missing identity still follows the original capture path and produces its original ERR. A permanently incomplete tracked graph remains NOT_READY, never PASS.

`CancelReplicaPublication` drops the held callback and keeps a removal fence. `CompleteReplicaRemoval` records completion of the original world cleanup; the fence is released only after the original registry removal is also visible. A publication exception emits ERR, latches the fault and is never retried. A reentrant destroy during publication retains its fence. Distinct container lifetime scopes have distinct ledgers. A changed `Campaign.Current` object reference invalidates old callbacks, even when a loaded campaign has the same string identity. Disposing the old session prevents late calls from publishing through its ledger.
